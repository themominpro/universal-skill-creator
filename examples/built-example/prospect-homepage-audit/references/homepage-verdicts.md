# Homepage Verdicts

## Pitch

Use Pitch only when the homepage itself, seen in the browser, looks weak, rushed, or unfinished. Examples of valid evidence, in plain words:

- Text-only hero with no product visual
- Near-placeholder page with no product description or call to action
- Blank hero plus a login panel, with a dated template feel
- Confusing banner or banner that contradicts the main offer

## Skip

Use Skip when the homepage already looks polished and complete, even when funding, hiring, or another trigger looks strong. A strong trigger does not override a good homepage.

## Unknown

Use Unknown when the homepage cannot be opened, or no verified website exists. Do not guess a website address. Do not reuse an old verdict for a site that may have changed; re-check it first.

## Evidence Rule

Evidence must name what was seen, not what was inferred. "Text-only hero, no product visual, right side empty" is evidence. "Probably needs a redesign" is not evidence.
